Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

TEAO Canada ("we," "our," or "us") operates the TEAO Canada mobile application (the "Service"). We are fully committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure, transparent user experience.

Privacy-First Design: Any personal features, logs, schedules, or task data you create inside the TEAO Canada mobile application are stored strictly locally on your own mobile device. We do not host remote databases for this personal content, and we cannot access it.

Please note that while your active user content stays on your device, this app utilizes select third-party industry tools to serve contextual advertisements and analyze aggregate performance metrics to support our services.

1. Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party Services

To help us support, maintain, and continuously improve our Service, we integrate specialized third-party developer software development kits (SDKs) provided by Google LLC. These tools automatically compile specific technical metadata from your device when the app is running:

  • Google Analytics (Firebase): Tracks aggregated usage patterns (e.g., screen navigation flows, session durations, and performance diagnostics like crash reports). This information helps us optimize software stability and design, and does not personally identify you.
  • Google Ads (AdMob): Facilitates the distribution of targeted advertisements within the app layout. Google may process unique advertising identifiers and approximate geolocational variables to distribute relevant marketing materials.

Types of Metadata Automatically Processed:

  • Hardware variables (Operating system versions, device model names, and manufacturer brands).
  • Unique mobile device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or iOS Identifier for Advertisers).
  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (utilized solely to gauge general, regional device location).
  • Interactive parameters (internal clicks, views registered on advertising media, and application launch metrics).

For comprehensive details on how Google processes, secures, and handles this infrastructure data, please consult the official policy webpage at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

2. User Controls and Data Tracking Opt-Out Options

You reserve full rights to restrict or opt out of personalized digital advertising tracking arrays across your device systems at any given time via your system-level preferences:

Android Devices: Navigate through Settings > Privacy > Ads and engage the options to "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Reset advertising ID."

iOS Devices: Access Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to globally monitor and adjust tracking configurations for local apps.

3. Newsletter Subscriptions & External Web Connections

The Service provides an option to join our corporate newsletter. The mobile app itself does not record, process, or transmit your contact credentials for this feature.

When launching the subscription portal, the app securely directs you to our official external web servers. Any information you subsequently input during subscription registration (specifically your first name, last name, and active email address) is managed strictly in accordance with our web-based data protocols and Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

4. Regulatory Compliance

We manage organizational information systems in compliance with the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and respective regional/provincial privacy laws across Canada. Because your personal content files never leave your hardware, your individual records remain secure under your direct custody.

5. Children's Privacy Safeguards

Our services are not structured to attract or systematically gather information from users under the age of 13. Since local core data remains entirely bound to your device environment, no identifiers or user metrics belonging to minors are intentionally routed to our infrastructure.

6. Revisions to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy structural disclosures periodically to match changing technical architectures or legal requirements. Updates will be broadcast directly on this page alongside a modified "Last Updated" revision timestamp located at the header of this statement.
